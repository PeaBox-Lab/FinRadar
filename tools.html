User guide

How to use the calculator hub

Pick a tool, adjust the inputs and read the result alongside the formula and notes.

01

Enter realistic inputs

Default inputs are provided for convenience only. Replace them with your actual numbers, rates and tenure.

02

Read the formula

Every page states the exact calculation. This makes it easy to audit a result or compare two offers.

03

Stress-test the answer

Change the rate, inflation or return by a few points. The range matters more than one exact figure.

Important: These calculators are decision aids, not financial advice. Lender tariffs, insurer underwriting and tax rules can change the final number.
