Real Interest Rate (Saver's Yield) 5.45 % STRONG Positive means savers beat price rises; negative means money in the bank quietly loses value. Every 100bps of fall is a signal to lock FD rates before they drop further. 10Y G-Sec Yield − CPI Inflation Detail
Real Inflation Rate (Headline) 2.40 % MODERATE The single most quoted cost-of-living number. Below 2% is stable; 2-4% is the RBI comfort zone; above 4% erodes real wages and usually triggers rate hikes. Headline CPI growth (annual %) Detail
GDP Growth (Annual %) 7.57 % STRONG Above 6% is strong expansion; 3-6% is steady; below 3% is a slowdown worth watching for job and income stress. Real GDP growth, year over year Detail
GDP per Capita (Nominal) 2,702.48 USD WEAK Average economic output per person. It is the usual headline number global rankings use, but it hides inequality — always pair with the PPP gap. GDP ÷ population, in current US$ Detail
GDP per Capita, PPP 11,747.92 int$ MODERATE What the same output buys inside the country. The gap vs nominal tells you how much cheaper daily life is — which is why a ₹20 LPA salary in Jaipur buys more than in Mumbai. GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power Detail
PPP Gap (Nominal − PPP) -9,045.44 USD STRONG A large negative gap means local prices are far cheaper than the exchange rate implies — money goes further inside the country. The bigger the gap, the more a remote or migrant salary is worth relative to its dollar figure. Nominal GDP per capita − PPP GDP per capita Detail
Current Account Balance (% of GDP) — A surplus means more money flows in than out (exports, remittances). A deficit above -3% is a warning that the country depends on foreign capital to fund its imports. Trade balance + net transfers, as % of GDP Detail
FDI Inflows (% of GDP) — Foreign money building factories, offices and infrastructure. Above 2% signals confidence; near zero means capital is staying home or leaving. Foreign direct investment as % of GDP Detail
Debt Service Burden (% of Revenue) 11.30 % MODERATE Every rupee of interest is a rupee that cannot fund schools, hospitals or roads. Rising debt service crowds out development spending — watch it alongside the fiscal deficit. Interest paid on public debt ÷ total revenue Detail
Import Cover Ratio 16.39 months STRONG How many months the country can keep importing if all export earnings stopped. Below 3 months is a balance-of-payments crisis zone; 6+ months is comfortable. Forex reserves ÷ (annual imports ÷ 12) Detail
Reserve Adequacy Ratio 1.21 x MODERATE Can the country repay all debt due within a year? Above 1.0x means yes; below 1.0x means reserves alone cannot cover near-term obligations — a downgrade risk. Forex reserves ÷ short-term external debt Detail
Fiscal Deficit (% of GDP) 5.10 % MODERATE How much the government must borrow to fund its spending. Above 6% is typically inflationary and crowds out private investment; below 3% is the FRBM target. Government borrowing ÷ GDP Detail
Index of Industrial Production Growth -0.40 % WEAK The cleanest read on whether factories are actually producing more. IIP leads GDP by a quarter — a falling IIP today is a slower GDP tomorrow. YoY growth in industrial output (factory, mine, utility) Detail
GST Collections Growth 11.70 % STRONG GST is levied on consumption, so its growth is a high-frequency read on consumer and business spending. Below 5% growth often means households are pulling back. YoY growth in GST collections Detail
Digital Consumption Velocity (UPI Ticket) 1.40 USD/tx MODERATE The average amount Indians move per UPI payment. Rising ticket size means bigger-ticket transactions are going digital — a sign of formalisation and consumption upgrading. Track it with GST growth. UPI transaction value ÷ UPI volume Detail
