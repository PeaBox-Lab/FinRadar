KPIs — 3 metrics

KPILatestVerdictInsightFormula
Hospital Bed Density (per 1,000 people) 1.30 beds/1000 WEAK How many hospital beds exist for every 1,000 people. Below 2 is a capacity crisis — a pandemic or even a bad flu season overwhelms the system. WHO recommends ~5 per 1,000 for ageing populations. Total hospital beds ÷ population × 1,000 Detail
Full Immunization Coverage 76.00 % MODERATE Share of young children who received all basic vaccines. Below 85% means pockets of population remain vulnerable to outbreaks — measles, diphtheria, polio. Fully immunized children ÷ children aged 12-35 months × 100 Detail
Out-of-Pocket Health Expenditure Share 47.10 % MODERATE How much of all health spending comes directly from patients' pockets. Above 50% means a serious illness can bankrupt a family — a key driver of poverty and of demand for health insurance. Household out-of-pocket spending ÷ total health spending × 100 Detail
