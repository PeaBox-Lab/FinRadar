Monthly snapshots

Curated datasets transcribed from official publications. Every figure on these pages carries a static-snapshot badge and a visible Last Updated month.

exchanges Static Snapshot • Updated Monthly (Not Live)Last Updated: November 2026

Global exchange fundamentals

Country-level World Bank WDI series are used as transparent proxies where single-exchange annual series are not published. Exchange-level WFE figures are month-end snapshots and are labelled with their exact period. Country coverage is explicitly stated; exchange-specific index levels and returns are included only where the index provider or exchange published them.

Coverage: 2020-2025 annual country series; 2023-2025 month-end WFE snapshots · 35 metrics · Source: World Bank / World Federation of Exchanges

Global GDP, GDP per capita and inflation (World Bank WDI)

Figures are transcribed directly from the World Bank API for each country and year. Null API observations are omitted rather than filled. These annual series are a static companion to the live global feeds; they are not monthly observations.

Coverage: Annual 2020-2025 for India, United States, China, Germany, United Kingdom, Japan and World · 21 metrics · Source: World Bank

auto Static Snapshot • Updated Monthly (Not Live)Last Updated: November 2026

India domestic vehicle sales (SIAM)

Figures are transcribed exactly from SIAM's published domestic-sales table. Each fiscal year is labelled with SIAM's own period. No estimated, interpolated or derived sales figures are included.

Coverage: FY2019-20 to FY2023-24 (April-March fiscal years) · 3 metrics · Source: Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM)

loans Static Snapshot • Updated Monthly (Not Live)Last Updated: November 2026

RBI bank housing credit outstanding

Figures are the published March outstanding amounts for bank credit to housing. Values since July 2023 include the impact of a non-bank merger; RBI's ex-merger figures are shown separately in the notes.

Coverage: March outstanding, 2021-2025 · 2 metrics · Source: Reserve Bank of India

India top-7-cities residential sales (JLL REIS)

Annual residential unit sales across India's top seven cities, transcribed from JLL's published report. JLL counts apartments only and excludes row houses, villas and plotted developments.

Insurance Static Snapshot • Updated Monthly (Not Live)Last Updated: November 2026

IRDAI insurance sector KPIs

Every figure is transcribed from an official IRDAI annual-report table or the report's Bird's Eye View. FY2019-20 comparatives come from IRDAI Annual Report 2020-21 because the scanned FY2019-20 report has no usable text layer. Penetration and density are Swiss Re figures reproduced by IRDAI and are labelled as such. Period labels follow IRDAI's own April-March fiscal-year convention, including tables that print calendar-style year headings.

Coverage: FY2019-20 to FY2024-25 · 11 metrics · Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)
