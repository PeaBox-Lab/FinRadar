FinRadar — Multi-Page Site Final Summary
============================================

PAGES: 96 HTML files
- index.html — Dashboard with enhanced nav + category cards
- search.html — Full-text search across 36 facts + 35 KPIs
- sources.html — Data source catalog
- 14 category pages — Central, states, financials, geo, transport, economy, agriculture, energy, health, education, welfare, environment, commerce, housing
- 36 fact detail pages — With source/scale/why it matters/insight/trend
- 35 KPI deep-dive pages — With formula, verdict badge, insight, source fields
- 8 historical trend pages — Canvas line chart + data table

NAVIGATION (index page)
- Primary: Dashboard, Search, Sources, Central Govt, States, Financials, Geo, Transport
- Dropdown: All 14 sectors (Central Govt, States, Financials, Geo, Transport, Economy, Agriculture, Energy & Power, Health, Education, Welfare & Employment, Environment & Climate, Trade & Commerce, Housing & Urban)
- "Explore by category" section: 14 cards with KPI counts, fact counts, descriptions, View buttons

DATA COVERAGE
- 36 facts across 12 sectors (central, states, financials, geo, transport, economy, agriculture, energy, health, education, welfare, environment, commerce, housing)
- 35 KPIs with derived metrics, ratios, velocities, composites
- 8 historical trend series (5+ years each)
- All data sourced from: World Bank, RBI DBIE, MoSPI, NPCI, GST portal, MNRE, Census, API Setu, MGNREGA, IMF, FRED

EACH PAGE INCLUDES:
- Data API origins (where the number comes from)
- Analytical verdicts (Strong/Moderate/Weak or on/near/off target)
- Plain-English insights (what the number means for policy/economy/people)
- Formulas (how each derived metric is calculated)
- Source fields (specific API columns/indicators)
- Historical trend embeds (multi-year time series)
- Searchable index entries
