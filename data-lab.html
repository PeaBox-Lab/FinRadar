Computed metrics

Innovative derived KPIs

These are not published by any single authority. Each card shows the exact inputs and formula so you can reproduce or challenge the number.

Per-capita growth spread

Favourable
6.68 pp

Formula: Real GDP growth − population growth

Definition: How much of headline growth remains after accounting for a growing population.

User guide: A wider spread means average output per person is rising faster. It is an approximation of GDP-per-capita growth.

Sources: World Bank NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG, World Bank SP.POP.GROW · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Demographic dividend index

Watch
53.5 index

Formula: Working-age share (%) × literacy rate (%) ÷ 100

Definition: Potential productive capacity from the size and education of the working-age population.

User guide: This is potential, not realised, capacity. Read it with employment and job-quality data before calling it a dividend.

Sources: World Bank SP.POP.1564.TO.ZS, World Bank SE.ADT.LITR.ZS · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Urbanisation productivity ratio

Interpret with care
21.3 ×

Formula: GDP per capita growth (%) ÷ change in urban share (pp)

Definition: Output growth generated for each percentage-point shift of population into towns and cities.

User guide: A high ratio can mean productive cities — or that urbanisation is simply too slow. Always inspect both inputs.

Sources: World Bank NY.GDP.PCAP.KD.ZG, World Bank SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Credit–savings gap

Interpret with care
11.5 pp of GDP

Formula: Domestic credit to private sector (% GDP) − gross domestic savings (% GDP)

Definition: How far private credit extends beyond the nation's own savings, indicating reliance on other funding sources.

User guide: A widening gap can support investment but may signal leverage. Compare across a full cycle, not a single year.

Sources: World Bank FS.AST.PRVT.GD.ZS, World Bank NY.GDS.TOTL.ZS · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Private credit per person

Context
1,190 USD

Formula: Domestic credit (% GDP) × GDP per capita (current US$) ÷ 100

Definition: Approximate financial depth available per resident.

User guide: Use it to compare financial access across countries; it does not show how credit is distributed.

Sources: World Bank FS.AST.PRVT.GD.ZS, World Bank NY.GDP.PCAP.CD · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

External resilience score

Watch
48.0 0–100

Formula: Average z-score of FDI (% GDP), current account (% GDP) and reserve cover (months), scaled to 0–100

Definition: Composite of the three external buffers that help an economy absorb a sudden stop in capital flows.

User guide: A score above 50 means the three buffers are above their own long-run averages; it is a relative, not absolute, measure.

Sources: World Bank BX.KLT.DINV.WD.GD.ZS, BN.CAB.XOKA.GD.ZS, FI.RES.TOTL.MO · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Peer growth spread

Favourable
3.57 pp

Formula: India GDP growth − average GDP growth of Vietnam, Indonesia, Brazil and Mexico

Definition: How much faster or slower India is growing than a basket of large emerging economies.

User guide: A positive spread suggests India is gaining relative ground; check the exchange rate and FDI before extrapolating.

Sources: World Bank India + 4 peer GDP series · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Global inflation gap

Interpret with care
-0.64 pp

Formula: India CPI inflation − world inflation

Definition: The part of domestic inflation that is above the global price trend.

User guide: A positive gap points to domestic cost or demand pressure; a negative gap suggests imported disinflation.

Sources: World Bank FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG (IND and WLD) · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Pollution intensity of growth

Under pressure
0.89 ×

Formula: Growth in PM2.5 (%) ÷ real GDP growth (%)

Definition: Change in air pollution associated with each unit of economic growth.

User guide: Below zero means air quality improved while the economy grew — a sign of decoupling. Values above one mean growth remains pollution-heavy.

Sources: World Bank EN.ATM.PM25.MC.M3, NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Digital growth elasticity

Interpret with care
1.31 ×

Formula: GDP per capita growth (%) ÷ change in internet users (% of population, pp)

Definition: Output growth associated with each percentage-point increase in internet adoption.

User guide: A high value can mean digital adoption is translating into output, or that adoption has stalled while the economy grew.

Sources: World Bank IT.NET.USER.ZS, NY.GDP.PCAP.KD.ZG · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

FX–real-rate gap

Favourable
-1.38 pp

Formula: Annual change in INR/USD exchange rate − real interest rate

Definition: Currency depreciation not offset by the real return offered to savers.

User guide: A positive gap flag means rupee weakness outpaces the real reward for holding rupee assets.

Sources: World Bank PA.NUS.FCRF, FR.INR.RINR · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Growth tailwind ratio

Favourable
2.59 ×

Formula: India GDP growth ÷ world GDP growth

Definition: How many times the global growth rate India is expanding.

User guide: Ratios above one indicate India is outpacing the world cycle; moderate ratios can reflect a global boom lifting all economies.

Sources: World Bank NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG (IND and WLD) · Latest year: 2025 · Annual

Global definitions & guides

Plain-English explanations of the indicators, units and caveats used in the global sections.

Central bank policy rate

Definition: The benchmark rate a central bank sets or targets to steer money-market conditions.

User guide: Compare a country's own rate across time. Instruments differ (repo, deposit, lending rate), so levels are not directly comparable.

Source: Bank for International Settlements ↗

Debt service ratio

Definition: Principal and interest payments divided by the borrower's income.

User guide: It measures repayment burden rather than the stock of debt. A high ratio can appear before defaults even when debt levels look manageable.

Source: Bank for International Settlements ↗

Credit gap

Definition: The percentage deviation of the credit-to-GDP ratio from its long-run trend.

User guide: Treat it as a financial-stability signal, not a timing tool. The Basel Committee uses it as a reference for countercyclical buffers.

Source: Bank for International Settlements ↗

PPP price-level ratio

Definition: PPP conversion factor divided by the market exchange rate.

User guide: It approximates how expensive a country is relative to the United States. It is not a city-level cost-of-living index.

Source: World Bank Open Data ↗

Gender wage gap

Definition: Female wages divided by male wages for comparable employees.

User guide: A value of 1 means parity. The World Bank series covers full-time dependent employees aged 15–64 and excludes many informal workers.

Source: World Bank Open Data ↗

Insurance penetration

Definition: Direct insurance premiums as a share of GDP.

User guide: It measures the size of the market relative to the economy. It does not say anything about claim settlement quality or insurer solvency.

Source: World Bank Global Financial Development Database ↗

Bank capital and NPLs

Definition: Capital to assets shows the equity cushion; non-performing loans show the share of loans in distress.

User guide: Read both together. Strong capital with rising NPLs means the sector can absorb losses but may still tighten lending.

Source: World Bank Open Data ↗

Central government debt

Definition: Gross central-government debt as a share of GDP.

User guide: Compare with the real interest rate and the currency of issuance. Gross debt is different from net debt.

Source: World Bank Open Data ↗

Equity market development

Definition: Market capitalisation relative to GDP and the turnover ratio of domestic shares.

User guide: Capitalisation rises with valuations as well as listings; turnover shows how actively shares trade relative to market size.

Source: World Bank Open Data ↗

Pink Sheet commodity prices

Definition: World Bank monthly nominal commodity prices in US dollars, with units specific to each commodity.

User guide: These are monthly benchmarks, not live quotes. Gold and silver are quoted per troy ounce, base metals per metric tonne and oil per barrel.

Source: World Bank Commodity Markets ↗

Why there is no global steel price

Definition: The Pink Sheet does not publish a single worldwide steel price; iron ore is its closest upstream benchmark.

User guide: Steel prices are regional and grade-specific. Use iron ore for the raw-material signal and a market quote for a specific region or grade.

Source: World Bank Commodity Markets ↗

Source scan

Discovery catalog

Official portal metadata is catalogued here; the series actually fetched are published above. This is a curated scan, not an exhaustive crawl of every dataset.

  • World Bank Open Data — Macro & National Accounts The backbone for cross-country macro comparisons and India's headline GDP, inflation, PPP and external-sector series. Open · no key required
  • data.gov.in (OGD Platform) — Macro & National Accounts India's national open-data platform. The widest catalogue of state- and district-level government datasets. API key (free registration); sample key for development
  • API Setu — Macro & National Accounts The government's API gateway. Useful for identity, document and transactional lookups rather than time-series statistics. Partner registration (scoped keys)
  • RBI Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) — Money, Prices & Markets The primary source for Indian monetary, banking and external-sector series. Used for real interest rates, reserve adequacy and bank health KPIs. Open portal; bulk downloads
  • NPCI Statistics — Money, Prices & Markets The definitive read on India's digital-payment velocity, ticket sizes and tolling volumes. Open monthly statistics
  • GST Portal — Collections — Fiscal & Government Finance A high-frequency proxy for consumption and formalisation, and the largest own-revenue source for states. Open monthly press releases
  • MoSPI — National Statistics — Money, Prices & Markets The official source for India's inflation, industrial production, national accounts and employment statistics. Open downloads
  • CEA / POSOCO Power Data — Energy & Power Near-real-time electricity demand and generation — one of the best proxies for industrial activity. Open daily / monthly reports
  • MNRE Renewable Capacity — Energy & Power Tracks India's energy transition against the 50% non-fossil capacity target for 2030. Open monthly progress reports
  • MoRTH / NHAI Transport Data — Transport & Logistics The source for road density, vehicle ownership and digital tolling across states. Open annual reports
  • Indian Railways Statistics — Transport & Logistics Freight and passenger trends for the world's fourth-largest rail network. Open annual statistical statements
  • DGCA Civil Aviation Statistics — Transport & Logistics Domestic aviation traffic — a leading indicator of business travel and premium consumption. Open monthly traffic reports
  • MoHFW / NFHS Health Data — Health, Education & Welfare The census of Indian health capacity, immunisation and household health spending. Open reports / microdata on request
  • UDISE+ School Education — Health, Education & Welfare School-level enrolment and teacher data used for the pupil-teacher ratio and education KPIs. Open reports / dashboards
  • MGNREGA MIS — Health, Education & Welfare Rural wage demand under the employment guarantee — a real-time distress signal. Open MIS reports
  • Census of India — Geo, Environment & Climate The demographic base layer for population density, urbanisation and literacy KPIs. Open tables
  • MoEF&CC Environment Data — Geo, Environment & Climate Land, forest and wetlands statistics behind the environment and geo KPIs. Open reports
  • NHB / RBI Housing Data — Housing & Urban House-price and affordability series for the housing KPIs. Open reports
  • IMF DataMapper / WEO — Macro & National Accounts Cross-country fiscal and debt comparables, plus forward-looking macro forecasts. Open
  • FRED (St. Louis Fed) — Money, Prices & Markets Global rates and commodity benchmarks used for context alongside Indian data. Open API key (free)
  • ECB Data Portal — EXR — Money, Prices & Markets Official daily euro reference rates used for the FX table and derived INR/USD cross rate. Open · no key
  • BIS Statistics API v2 — Money, Prices & Markets Curated BIS dataflows for policy rates, debt-service burdens, credit gaps and effective exchange rates. Open · no key
  • WTO Timeseries API — Trade & Commerce Annual merchandise exports and imports with the world for eight major trading economies. Open · no key
  • World Bank Pink Sheet — Macro & National Accounts Official monthly nominal commodity prices with per-commodity units, parsed from the published workbook. Open · no key
  • Yahoo Finance market quotes — Money, Prices & Markets Delayed market-reference snapshots clearly labelled as secondary data; never presented as official statistics. Open · no key · delayed

User guide

How to use this data responsibly

  1. Read the unit and frequency. Annual World Bank figures are not monthly snapshots; Census figures are decadal.
  2. Use the definition before comparing. Similar labels can use different age ranges or methodologies.
  3. Check the formula on derived KPIs. They combine published inputs; they are interpretations, not official statistics.
  4. Treat correlations as clues. A coefficient shows co-movement over the stated period, not cause and effect.
  5. Refresh cadence. The separate enrichment workflow runs every night at 03:15 UTC and regenerates this page.
  6. Access boundary. Public-key sources are limited; a personal data.gov.in key and APIsetu consumer credentials unlock larger volumes.
