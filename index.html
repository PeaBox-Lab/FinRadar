The full KPI list — every metric we calculate

Definitions written for decision-makers, not mathematicians.

MetricWhat it means
Real Interest Rate (Saver's Yield) The 10-YG-Sec Yield minus the headline inflation index. If this is positive, savers are actually earning more than prices are rising. Negative means your money quietly loses value while you keep it in a bank.
Real Inflation Rate (Headline) The official Consumer Price Index growth, in percent. What people usually mean by “it is getting more expensive.”
Regional Inflation Spread The gap between the national CPI and the average CPI of food and housing — it shows whether the national average is hiding a much larger cost spike in your city.
Digital Consumption Velocity (UPI Ticket) The average value per UPI transaction, alongside GST collections growth. If value moves faster than volume, India is transacting bigger amounts in fewer moments — a fast-growing digital economy.
Import Cover & Reserve Adequacy Ratio Forex reserves divided by roughly one month of imports. If it falls, the country can import less, month to month.
Reserve Adequacy Ratio FX reserves as a share of short-term debt. Above 100% means the reserve pile covers the near-term debt bill.
Debt Service Burden Interest paid on public debt as a share of GDP — or of total government revenue. When this rises, the tax bill that could have gone to schools or health instead pays interest.
GDP per Capita (Nominal) The average economic output per person, in US dollars, as the World Bank reports it.
Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) Gap The difference between nominal GDP per capita and PPP-adjusted GDP per capita — plus the implied PPP conversion rate. This is why a ₹20 LPA salary in Delhi buys a different life than the same salary in Jaipur.
GDP Growth (Annual %) How fast the economy is growing, year over year.
Current Account Balance (% of GDP) Trade balance plus transfers such as remittances, as a share of GDP. Negative means more money flows out of the country than in.
FDI Inflows (% of GDP) Foreign money flowing into the country for projects, expansions and asset buys — the main side of capital flows besides remittances.
Personal Remittances (% of GDP) Money sent back home from abroad — for families, small businesses and some labor mobility.
Government Debt-to-GDP Total public debt relative to the size of the economy — and compared to peers, debt is easy to see.
GDP per Capita, PPP (Current International $) GDP adjusted for what the same number of dollars actually buys in each country.
Index of Industrial Production Growth How fast factories, mines and utilities are actually growing, not just how fast money changes hands.
Power Demand Growth The growth in electricity used — a rough real-time check of whether factories and industry are turning on the lights or turning some off.
Overall Inflation Trend (Headline) Where the headline inflation is pointing — trending up means the cost of living keeps getting worse month by month.

How to read the India Macro Cycle Score

Current India score:

The score blends GST collections, UPI value, power demand, IIP, inflation, the repo rate and GDP growth into a single 0–100 figure.

  • 80–100 — Expansion: growth usually holds; savings can target yield, not safety. Watch UPI value for signs of stretched consumption.
  • 55–79 — Rotational Slowdown: if the driver is inflation, short-duration FDs and debt allocation tell faster stories than longer ones.
  • 30–54 — Recovery: dig into GST and UPI growth; private consumption signals are still lagging the headline number.
  • 0–29 — Contracted: step back to the headline macro; consider downside protection and revisiting risk policy on term debt.
